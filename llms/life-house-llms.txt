# Life House

> Life House was a vertically integrated hotel software, brand and management company (founded 2017,
> Miami Beach, Florida) that built its own back-of-house operating system — property management,
> revenue management, digital marketing and financial operations — for independent hotels, operated
> 50+ properties across the US, Mexico and Canada, and sold the platform to third-party hoteliers.
> In December 2024 it combined its management portfolio with Lark Hotels to form Lark Hospitality
> (Lark holding the controlling interest) and spun its AI revenue-management platform out as Diamo.
> Life House published no public API, SDK, webhook surface or developer portal; it was a consumer of
> hotel APIs (notably the Mews Open API) rather than a publisher of one.
